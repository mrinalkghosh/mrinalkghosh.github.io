Mrinalkanti Ghosh

Pronounced mree-naal kaanti ghosh

I’m a postdoctoral fellow at the Indian Institute of Science, mentored by Anand Louis. I study approximation algorithms and hardness of approximation for constraint satisfaction problems, matrix norms, and polynomial optimization.

I received my PhD from TTIC in 2025, advised by Madhur Tulsiani. I did my M.Tech. at IIT Kanpur with Satyadev Nandakumar.
